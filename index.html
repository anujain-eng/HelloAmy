Salon Policies

Our Rules & Guidelines

We ask every guest to follow these simple guidelines so everyone can enjoy a peaceful, safe, and comfortable experience.

🚭

No Smoking or Drinking

Smoking and alcohol are strictly not allowed anywhere on the premises to keep the environment clean and safe for all guests and staff.

📅

Appointments Required

All services require a booking in advance. Walk-ins are welcome based on availability, but appointments are always prioritized.

⏰

Please Arrive on Time

Guests who arrive more than 15 minutes late may need to reschedule their appointment. We respect everyone's time.

👧

Children Must Be Supervised

Parents and guardians are responsible for their children at all times. Please keep little ones close and calm to maintain a relaxing atmosphere.

🍱

No Outside Food or Drinks

To keep our salon clean and hygienic, we ask guests not to bring outside food or beverages. Enjoy treats from our in-house snack shop instead!

🤝

Respectful Conduct

Kindness goes a long way. We expect all guests to treat our staff and fellow guests with courtesy and respect at all times.

📵

Phone Etiquette

Please keep phone calls quiet and brief while inside the salon. Loud calls can disrupt the peaceful environment for other guests.

💳

Payment at Time of Service

All services must be paid for upon completion. We accept cash and major cards. Deposits may be required for certain bookings.

Your Protection

Safety & Security

Your safety is our top priority. Our building is equipped with the latest security systems to keep you protected at all times.

🔒

Secure Entry System

Our doors have electronic locks and controlled access points. Only authorized guests and staff can enter the building.

📹

24/7 Security Cameras

CCTV surveillance throughout all common areas and hallways to ensure the safety of everyone in the building.

🚨

Emergency Systems

Fire alarms, sprinklers, emergency exits on every floor, and a fully stocked first aid station for any situation.

👮

Trained Staff

All employees are trained in emergency procedures, first aid, and customer safety protocols.

🔑

Secure Storage

Personal lockers available for guests to safely store their belongings while they enjoy our services.

🧯

Fire Safety

Fire extinguishers on every floor, clearly marked exit routes, and regular safety inspections and drills.
